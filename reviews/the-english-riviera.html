The English Riviera

Metronomy

2011

Reviewed 8 August 2026 — 15 years late to the party.

★★★★

One of my favourite albums of the 2010's, although forgettable enough that I get to be pleasantly surpised as if hearing it for the first time every 6 months or so. That was meant to be a compliment, but it also makes me feel like a bit of a dick for not appreciating it more.

You ever get really into a band, and then realise they don't really tour anymore. Or you live on a tiny isolated island in the middle of the pacific and they are unlikely to tour your country cause the cost of living effects everyone?

The Album is dope, great for a summer drive, or a summer anything really. I think the album is a bit of a guilty pleasure for me, but the kind of guilty pleasure that I'll do again and again. Also, I initially misspelt "Guilty" and now have a new phrase. "Quilty Pleasure": where you cuddle up under and blanket and enjoy something that not everyone would enjoy. The blanket is essential, otherwise you are just being a niche dick.

Where was I? I really enjoy "The Bay" and "The Look" I might have enjoyed other songs too if they were prefaced by "The"
