The Car

Arctic Monkeys

2022

Reviewed 18 July 2026 — 4 years late to the party.

★★★½

The Arctic Monkeys have been gradually moving away from their hard driving stadium anthems and into a more loungey, poppy sound. The Car is a continuation of the lounge asthetic that was first explored through Alex Turner's side and solo projects, and then plunged into by the band on Tranquility Base Hotel and Casino. The Car is a more refined version of that sound, with a more cohesive sound and a more focused direction.

The irony of this album is that it is so chill, I wouldn't listen to it while driving. Side note, the album artwork for The Car is a gorgeous photograh taken by Drummer, Matt Helders. Gives dystopian vibes.
