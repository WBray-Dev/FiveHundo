All The Young Dudes

Mott the Hoople

1972

Reviewed 1 August 2026 — 54 years late to the party.

★★★

We all know the story of David Bowie and Mott the Hoople, but what about the story of the album that saved Mott the Hoople's career? All The Young Dudes is a classic rock album that has stood the test of time. It is a must listen for any fan of classic rock.

Come for the Bowie, stay for the Hoople. Put on your stolen Marks and Sparks threads and spend the afternoon taking in the nostalgic Bowie sound.
