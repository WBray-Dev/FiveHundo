About FiveHundo

FiveHundo is a project about doing everything in extremes. I get a nice CD player for my birthday, I want to listen to the greatest 500 albums of all time. I listen to the greatest 500 albums of all time, I want to blog about it. I blog about it I may as well have my own website. If I'm going to have my own website, I better learn how to code.

The hardest part was starting, but now I have had some success the momentum appears to be building. All I have to do now is listen to 500 albums (approx 2 weeks of music) and write a review (sometimes two) for each one.

I come up with the ideas I want on the website and then I ask Chat GPT to build segments of the code, in no way do I believe I have mastered coding, however I have learnt the basics of web development and I believe I could get a basic website up and running with a little less help from Chat GPT. My therapist would be so proud of all these 'I' statements.

No grand claims. No professional music critic pretending to have all the answers. Just 500 records and 500 reviews.
